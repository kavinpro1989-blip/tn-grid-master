🎓 Tamil Nadu Grid Master: Classroom Presentation Guide

☀️ 1. Daytime Solar Peak (12:00 Noon) — Kamuthi Solar Park

Kamuthi in Ramanathapuram is one of India's largest solar installations (648 MW).

  • 100% Clean: Generates electricity with zero carbon emissions ($0.00\text{ tons CO}_2$).
  • High Efficiency (95%): Short transmission paths directly into high-voltage lines minimize losses.
  • Zero Brownouts: All 6 town buildings (School, Clinic, Farm, Homes) receive full $230\text{V}$ power.

🏭 2. Evening Coal Peak (19:30) — Mettur Thermal Power Station

When the sun sets, electricity demand jumps as everyone turns on lights, fans, and televisions. The state must fire up coal boilers at Mettur (1,440 MW).

  • High Pollution: Releases thick particulate soot and $0.92\text{ tons CO}_2$ per MWh.
  • Severe Heat Loss (>30%): Traversing long transmission distances causes electricity to evaporate as heat ($I^2R$ Joule loss).
  • Voltage Sag & Brownouts: Downstream houses (Houses 4, 5, 6) suffer power drops, dimming bulbs, and blackouts.
