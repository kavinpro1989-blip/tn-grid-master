# 🎙️ Mithran's Speech — TN Grid Master
### Role: Anchor & Energy Story Lead
**Speaking Time:** `0:00 – 1:00` (60 Seconds)  
**Target Audience:** Parents, Teachers, and Students (NO JUDGES)  
**Stage Position:** Stand on the left of the booth table, smiling warmly.  

---

## 🧠 Mithran's Anti-Shyness & Stage Tricks
* **The Forehead Trick:** If looking into people's eyes makes you nervous, look directly at their foreheads. To them, it looks like confident eye contact, but you will feel completely relaxed!
* **The Table Anchor:** Keep both hands lightly resting on the edge of the display table or hold a small cue card. This stops nervous hand shaking.
* **The Quick Hand-Off:** You only talk for 60 seconds! It's a quick sprint, and then Faaris takes over with the hardware.

---

## 🎤 Mithran's Word-for-Word Full Speech (60 Seconds)

*(Wait for teachers or parents to approach your booth. Smile and speak in a clear, friendly voice)*

> "Hello! Good morning, teachers, parents, and friends! Welcome to our booth!
> 
> Quick question for everyone: Have you ever noticed that whenever power cuts or dim lights happen at home, it usually happens right between 6:30 and 8:00 in the evening?
> 
> Most people don't know this, but our home state, **Tamil Nadu**, is actually India's #1 green energy champion!
> 
> We are blessed with the mighty **Muppandal wind corridor** in Kanyakumari—one of the largest onshore wind farms on Earth—and the massive **Kamuthi Solar Park** in Ramanathapuram, spanning over 2,500 acres. On bright, breezy afternoons, over **60%** of Tamil Nadu's electricity is generated completely clean and free from the sun and wind!
> 
> But here is the big everyday puzzle:
> 
> **At 6:30 in the evening, the sun sets, the wind drops, and at that exact moment, 80 million people return home from work and school, turning on their lights, TV, and air conditioners!**
> 
> If power generation and consumer demand are not matched second-by-second, the entire electrical grid collapses into blackouts or low-voltage brownouts!
> 
> To demonstrate how modern smart grids solve this, our team created **TN Grid Master: A Cyber-Physical Smart Grid Digital Twin** that bridges real benchtop electronics with a live software simulation.
> 
> To show you how our physical smart grid bench works, here is our Hardware Lead, **Faaris**!"

*(Mithran smiles and gestures with an open hand toward Faaris)*
