## 🎬 3. Minute-by-Minute Action Script

### 🕒 0:00 – 1:00 | PHASE 1: MITHRAN'S WELCOME & HOOK
* **Visual Action:** Mithran smiles warmly at arriving teachers, parents, and students. Both hands rest lightly on the table.
* **What Mithran Says:**
  > "Hello! Good morning, teachers, parents, and friends! Welcome to our booth!
  > 
  > Quick question: Have you ever noticed that whenever power cuts happen at home, it usually happens between 6:30 and 8:00 in the evening?
  > 
  > Most people don't know this, but our home state, **Tamil Nadu**, is India's #1 green energy champion! We have the **Muppandal wind corridor** in Kanyakumari and the **Kamuthi Solar Park** in Ramanathapuram (648 MW). On bright afternoons, over **60%** of Tamil Nadu's electricity is generated clean from the sun and wind!
  > 
  > But here is the big puzzle: **At 6:30 PM, the sun sets, the wind drops, and 80 million people return home, switching on lights, TVs, and air conditioners!**
  > 
  > If supply and demand aren't matched second-by-second, the grid collapses into blackouts!
  > 
  > To solve this, our team created **TN Grid Master: A Cyber-Physical Smart Grid Digital Twin**. To show you how our physical bench works, here is our Hardware Lead, **Faaris**!"
* **Handoff Action:** Mithran turns and gestures with an open palm to Faaris.

### 🕒 1:00 – 2:15 | PHASE 2: FAARIS'S HARDWARE & SENSORY DEMO
* **Visual Action:** Faaris steps forward, pointing to each hardware component on the desk.
* **What Faaris Says:**
  > "Thank you, Mithran! Welcome everyone! Right here on our desk is our **physical smart grid test bench**:
  > 1. *(Point)* **4.9V Solar Panel** connected to an Arduino Uno microcontroller.
  > 2. *(Point)* **Dual 5V Relays** that switch power lines between solar and backup grid.
  > 3. *(Point)* **5V Propeller Fan**, representing ventilation and home cooling loads.
  > 4. *(Point)* **16x2 LCD & LEDs**, showing live voltage and green energy percentage."
* **THE CLIMAX DEMO (1:45):**
  * *Faaris picks up the flashlight and shines it directly onto the solar panel.*
  > *"Look what happens when sunlight hits our panel! The relay clicks ON, our Green LED lights up, our fan spins at top speed on pure solar power, and the LCD confirms 100% clean power!"*
  * *Faaris turns off the flashlight.*
  > *"When the sun is gone, the relay clicks off, and our system smoothly switches to backup power. Now, to see how our software scales this across Tamil Nadu, here is our Software Lead, **Kavin**!"*
* **Handoff Action:** Faaris gestures toward Kavin and the laptop screen.
